# Badge and patch placement

<CadetResourceLayout>

**Draft for Commanding Officer review.**

This guide covers the Air Cadet dress jacket and the rank slip-ons used on the field uniform. Left and right always mean the cadet’s own left and right while wearing the garment.

## Start with the jacket shoulder titles

Centre one squadron shoulder title on each sleeve. Measure **2 cm** down from the upper shoulder seam to the **top edge of the badge**. See [Chapter 3, section 1 — Insignia guidelines](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-3.html), paragraph 3(c)(2), and [Chapter 3, annex H — Insignia placement](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-3/annex-h.html), Figures 3H-5 and 3H-6.

![A sleeve schematic shows the shoulder title centred with its top edge 2 cm below the upper shoulder seam, and the midpoint between the shoulder and elbow for ordinary sleeve rank.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/badge-placement/sleeve.svg)

## Check rank and earned insignia

- **Sleeve rank below WO2.** For issued rank insignia covered by the standard rule, centre it midway between the upper shoulder seam and the elbow. Chapter 3, section 1, paragraph 3(c)(3).
- **WO2 or WO1.** Paragraph 3(c)(3) describes a cuff measurement of 9 cm to the badge bottom. The Annex H figures and appointment wording need clarification before using the guide as a sewing template. Confirm the side and arrangement with staff.
- **Training level.** The highest earned training level badge goes on the left sleeve, with its bottom 9 cm above the top of the cuff. WO2 and WO1 omit this badge. Paragraph 3(c)(4).
- **Proficiency.** Earned proficiency badges are arranged above the left cuff. Use paragraph 3(c)(5) and Figure 3H-5 to confirm the layout for your awards.
- **Course, staff and exchange badges.** **Review required.** Paragraph 3(c)(7) says left arm, while Figure 3H-6 depicts course badges on the right. Ask staff to confirm the applicable direction before sewing.
- **Pilot badge.** For eligible cadets, the highest issued sewn pilot qualification is centred 0.5 cm above the left breast pocket. Paragraph 3(c)(1).
- **C5 rank.** Use the issued rank slip-on for the field shirt. See [Chapter 2, section 2 — Appearance](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-2/section-2.html), paragraph 31, and [Chapter 6, annex L — Air Cadet training dress](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-6/annex-l.html), C5 row.

## Attach a badge

**Official requirement.** Use thread that matches the base cloth for sewn insignia. Avoid glue and iron-on attachment. The pilot insignia rule specifies nylon thread. See [Chapter 3, section 1 — Insignia guidelines](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-3.html), paragraphs 3(c)(1) and 3(d).

**Practical method.** Lay the sleeve flat, measure from the named seam and hold the badge in place with a temporary basting stitch. Check it while the jacket is worn, then sew around its edge. Take care to stitch through the outer fabric without catching the opposite side of the sleeve.

**Common mistakes.** Measuring to the centre when the rule names an edge, confusing wearer-left with viewer-left, or applying dress-jacket measurements to field clothing. Ask staff before attaching awards or an appointment badge you have just received.
