# Uniform dress standards

<CadetResourceLayout>

**Draft for Commanding Officer review.**

Use this guide to prepare your issued uniform when the squadron announces C1 or C5. An **order of dress** tells you which uniform and items to wear for an activity.

## C1 ceremonial dress

For Air Cadet C1, gather your wedge and authorized insignia or applicable authorized headdress, dress shirt, necktie, jacket with its belt, trousers with their belt, socks and dress boots. Annex C lists medals, pins and pilot insignia where applicable, as well as authorized accessories. Wear insignia and accessories according to your entitlement and event instructions. See [Chapter 6, annex C — Air Cadet ceremonial dress](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-6/annex-c.html), C1 row.

## C5 field training uniform

For Air Cadet C5, gather your authorized headdress, field shirt, rank slip-ons, authorized T-shirt, field pants and belt, socks and multi-purpose boots. Annex L lists headdress choices and allows sleeves extended or rolled above the elbows. The name tape is optional under the C5 row. C5A has its own list, including the T-shirt without the field shirt. Confirm which order was announced before packing. See [Chapter 6, annex L — Air Cadet training dress](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-6/annex-l.html), C5 and C5A rows.

## Before you leave

- **Official appearance checks.** Wear the appropriate rank slip-ons on the dress shirt and FTU where applicable. Wear a neat, tight knot with the C1 tie. Keep dress boots clean and shone and field boots clean with their unshone finish. See [Chapter 2, section 2 — Appearance](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-2/section-2.html), paragraphs 31 and 33–36.
- **Practical care.** Follow the garment care label. Empty pockets before washing, hang garments to dry as directed, and check seams and fasteners before the next activity. Ask staff about repairs or exchanges if something is missing or no longer fits.
- **Common mix-ups.** Check that you have the right boots and belt for the announced order. Read the activity notice again if it says C5A or authorizes different footwear.
