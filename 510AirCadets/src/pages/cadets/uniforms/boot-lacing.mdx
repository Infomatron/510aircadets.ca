# How to lace your boots

<CadetResourceLayout>

**Draft for Commanding Officer review.**

Check which boots the announced order calls for. The diagrams show the lace path through the eyelets, the holes along each side of the boot. Dashed lines show sections under the eyelet panels.

## Dress boots

**Official requirements.** Lace ankle boots with horizontal bars across the front. Keep them clean and shone. The sole and heel must be free of added metal fittings. See [Chapter 2, section 2 — Appearance](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-2/section-2.html), paragraph 35 and Figure 2-2-10.

1. Make a bar across the lowest pair of eyelets. Feed both tips in from outside and even up the lengths.
2. Bring the right end up under the right panel to the next hole. Bring it out there and take it across into the hole opposite.
3. Bring the other end up under its panel, skipping the row you just filled. Bring it out and form the next bar.
4. Continue alternating ends and adding bars. With an odd number of pairs, the official figure shows a final crossing near the top to finish the pattern.

![Dress boot eyelets with four straight bars across the tongue and dashed runs under the panels between rows.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/boot-lacing/dress.svg)

## Field training boots

**Official requirements.** Cross-lace multi-purpose boots and keep them clean. Blackening with polish is allowed and shining is prohibited. See [Chapter 2, section 2 — Appearance](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-2/section-2.html), paragraph 36 and Figure 2-2-10.

1. Run a bar across the lowest pair of eyelets and feed both tips in. Match their lengths.
2. Carry each end diagonally to the opposite side one row up and feed it out from underneath. Start with the left lace on the left boot and the right lace on the right boot, as shown in the official figure.
3. Repeat to the top, keeping each pair of crossings flat.

![Field boot eyelets with a starting bar and a series of X shapes formed by the two lace ends.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/boot-lacing/field.svg)

## Secure and check the laces

**Practical advice.** Work slack out from the bottom up and tie a secure bow. Keep loose ends clear of the sole and avoid tightening the laces until they pinch. Ask staff how to finish the issued boot if it has hooks or a different top closure. Brush off dirt before caring for the boot finish.

**Common mistakes.** Twisted bars, unequal ends, skipping a row in the cross-laced pattern, and shining field boots. Compare both boots before putting them away.
