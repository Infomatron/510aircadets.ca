# How to tie your C1 tie

<CadetResourceLayout>

**Draft for Commanding Officer review.**

Use this guide when wearing the tie with Air Cadet C1. All directions are from your own point of view.

## National requirements and 510 Squadron practice

The national instructions require a neat, tight knot and allow Windsor or Four in Hand. This tutorial teaches only the **Double Windsor**, the reported C1 practice at 510 Squadron, pending confirmation by the Commanding Officer. See [Chapter 2, section 2 — Appearance](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-2/section-2.html), paragraph 33 and Figure 2-2-9, and [Chapter 6, annex C — Air Cadet ceremonial dress](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/cadets-junior-canadian-rangers/cjcr-policy/cjcr-dress-instructions/chapter-6/annex-c.html).

## Tie the Double Windsor

Work in front of a mirror. The arrow shows the movement of the wide end. The illustrations are schematic and pair with the text.

### 1. Set the length

Drape the tie with the wide end on your right. Start with the wide tip roughly 30 cm below the narrow tip. Adjust the starting lengths for your height and tie.

![Step 1. Drape the tie with the wide end on your right. Start with the wide tip roughly 30 cm below the narrow tip. Adjust the starting lengths for your height and tie.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/tie/step-1.svg)

### 2. Cross in front

Bring the wide end across the front of the narrow end to your left. Hold the crossing with your fingers.

![Step 2. Bring the wide end across the front of the narrow end to your left. Hold the crossing with your fingers.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/tie/step-2.svg)

### 3. Make the first wrap

Lift the wide end through the neck opening from below. Let it fall on your left.

![Step 3. Lift the wide end through the neck opening from below. Let it fall on your left.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/tie/step-3.svg)

### 4. Pass behind

Move the wide end behind the narrow end to your right.

![Step 4. Move the wide end behind the narrow end to your right.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/tie/step-4.svg)

### 5. Make the second wrap

Lift the wide end on your right. Feed it down through the neck opening from the front and bring it out on your right.

![Step 5. Lift the wide end on your right. Feed it down through the neck opening from the front and bring it out on your right.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/tie/step-5.svg)

### 6. Make the front band

Bring the wide end across the front to your left. Leave a finger under the band to keep the opening clear.

![Step 6. Bring the wide end across the front to your left. Leave a finger under the band to keep the opening clear.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/tie/step-6.svg)

### 7. Thread the front band

Lift the wide end through the neck opening from below, then feed it down through the front band.

![Step 7. Lift the wide end through the neck opening from below, then feed it down through the front band.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/tie/step-7.svg)

### 8. Tighten and centre

Pull the wide end to shape the knot. Hold the narrow end and slide the knot up to your collar. Centre the triangle and lower the collar.

![Step 8. Pull the wide end to shape the knot. Hold the narrow end and slide the knot up to your collar. Centre the triangle and lower the collar.](/img/resources/cadets/uniforms/tie/step-8.svg)
## Check the result

As a practical fit check, aim for the wide tip to reach about the waistband or belt buckle when standing. The cited instructions do not specify this length. If the tip lands too high, start with more length on the wide side and retie. If the knot looks lopsided, check that you made both wraps and that the front band stayed flat.
